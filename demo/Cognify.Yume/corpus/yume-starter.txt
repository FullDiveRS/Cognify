# ============================================================================
#  yume-starter.txt — a starter training corpus for Yume.
#
#  Feed it to her:   dotnet run -- --train corpus/yume-starter.txt
#  or inside chat:   train corpus/yume-starter.txt
#
#  One utterance per line. Blank lines and lines starting with '#' are
#  ignored. Three kinds of lines:
#
#    facts        "birds are animals"      -> knowledge she reasons with
#    arithmetic   "1 + 1 = 2"              -> her math (taught, no calculator)
#    plain lines  "I love learning new..." -> SAYINGS: sentence shapes she
#                                             can speak in her own voice.
#                                             More sayings = more variety,
#                                             less repetition.
# ============================================================================
