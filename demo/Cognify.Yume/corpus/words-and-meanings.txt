# ============================================================================
#  words-and-meanings.txt — a dictionary Yume can be trained with.
#
#  Feed it to her:   --train corpus/words-and-meanings.txt
#  or inside chat:   train corpus/words-and-meanings.txt
#
#  One word per line, dictionary style:  <word> means <meaning>.
#  Every line becomes a real FACT (word, means, meaning) in her knowledge
#  base — so afterwards she can answer:
#
#      what does joy mean?        what is the meaning of grief?
#      define patience            what is a fox?
#
#  Blank lines and lines starting with '#' are ignored. Words were chosen
#  to be simple, warm and concrete — the kind of vocabulary worth growing.
# ============================================================================
